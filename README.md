# The Memory of Occupied Athens in the Urban Landscape
**Digital Mapping and Spatial Narrative Using Textual GIS Tools**

## 📌 Overview
This Master's thesis was conducted within the framework of the **M.Sc. in Digital Methods for the Humanities** at the **Athens University of Economics and Business (AUEB)**. 

The research explores the intersection of literature and the urban landscape of Athens during the Axis Occupation (1941–1944), using Zorz Sari’s novel *"When the Sun..."* (*«Όταν ο Ήλιος...»*) as a primary case study. Through **Textual GIS** methodologies, historical memory and literary testimony are georeferenced and transformed into an interactive spatial narrative.

## 🛠️ Methodology & Tools
- **Textual GIS & Spatial Analysis:** Georeferencing literary passages, extracting toponyms, and mapping places of memory across the urban area of Athens.
- **Digital Storytelling & Documentation:** Notion (structuring the spatial narrative, historical context, and visual documentation).
- **Literary & Historical Data:** Zorz Sari, *"When the Sun..."* (*«Όταν ο Ήλιος...»*), historical records, and spatial coordinates.

## 📂 Key Components
- **Toponym Extraction & Geolocation:** Identifying real-world Athenian landmarks, neighborhoods, and everyday routes depicted in the novel.
- **Spatial Narrative:** Connecting literary excerpts with historical context and modern urban geography.
- **Memory Mapping:** Visualizing how personal and collective trauma is inscribed onto the city's landscape.
